# Sample visual assets

Sample listings are fictional and clearly labelled. They are not real offers.

- `lamp.jpg`, `books.jpg`, `chair.jpg`: original CampusLoop editorial illustrations created for this project. These are illustrations, not photographs of offered goods.
- `croissants.jpg` (actually a loaf of bread): bread cutout from [Bedimcode responsive bakery website](https://github.com/bedimcode/responsive-bakery-website), `assets/img/home-bread.png`.
- `bowl.jpg`: food cutout from [Bedimcode responsive restaurant website](https://github.com/bedimcode/responsive-website-restaurant), `assets/img/plate1.png`.
- `plant.jpg`, `hero.jpg`: cactus cutout from [Bedimcode responsive plants website](https://github.com/bedimcode/responsive-plants-website), `assets/img/product1.png`.
